Community Energy Explorer

deprivation, civic fabric and grid capacity→a map

An interactive map of where community energy organisations exist in England, how that aligns with deprivation at small-area level, what wider community infrastructure exists that could be built on, and where the electricity grid has capacity for new local projects.

The data explorer scores every English local authority on need, meaning deprivation, and readiness, meaning civic fabric, nearby energy knowledge and grid headroom.
