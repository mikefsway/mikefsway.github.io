Without Fire

a Raspberry Pi→the look, sound and smell of a stove

Without Fire is a wood-burning stove simulator that aims to replicate in part the look, sounds, scent, and feel of a real stove. The motivation for this project was to help households with woodburners reduce air pollution while still enjoying their stove, or fireplace.

It is based around a Raspberry Pi computer, and this GitHub repository includes full details to let you replicate it, and improve it, yourself.
