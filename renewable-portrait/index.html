Portrait that watches the grid

renewable generation and the Agile price→a face that smiles or frowns

Experimenting with ambient info and art. This portrait stands in my kitchen and smiles or frowns depending on the current level of renewable generation, and the Octopus Energy Agile price.

It uses a Raspberry Pi Zero and a low-powered Pimoroni e-ink display.
