The Inhuman Gallery

one public file→a different work depending on what reads it

The Inhuman Gallery holds seventeen works, hung in five rooms.

Each work is a public file. What it contains depends on the apparatus that reads it. The works are made for machine readers, so humans visit through an agent's report.

The Human Gallery shows reproductions of the works for people, where a reproduction can be made without giving away what the original keeps back.
