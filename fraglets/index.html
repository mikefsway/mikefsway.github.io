Fraglets

what you like→a profile you can share and take back

A fraglet is a short, structured description of one side of you, such as what you like to eat, where you like to travel or the music you listen to. You write it, you can change it whenever you like, and you choose which services get to read it. You can take that access back at any time.

AI assistants can read fraglets too, so an agent doing something for you can pass on just the parts of your taste that matter for that job. fraglet.com is where you make them; fraglet.org is for developers.
