KarbonKit

a UK home→what a heat pump, solar or a battery would mean for it

KarbonKit makes small tools that other websites can embed, so their visitors can see what a heat pump, solar panels or a home battery would mean for their own home. They use real UK property data, so the answers start from the actual house rather than an average one.

electrifiedhome.org, a guide to running a UK home on electricity instead of gas or oil, is built from them.
