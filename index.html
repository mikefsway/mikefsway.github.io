I'm Mike Fell. This website is mainly a hub for my personal projects.

If you are looking for academic outputs and detail on my work as an energy researcher, please see my University College London page. I sometimes blog about topics like energy and technology, and post those on Medium.

Quite a bit of my work has some relation to energy and/or sound. Find out more about my sustainability projects, some based around Raspberry Pi, my cassette tape drawings, and sound and technology pieces.

Imagine a Venn diagram with circles for energy, society, technology, policy, open science, art, and sound/music. I'm interested in innovative projects that sit at the overlaps between two or more of these categories. If you have any ideas you would like to discuss, or opportunities to exhibit, write or speak that you think could be relevant, please contact me.
