The personal site of Mike Fell, energy researcher at UCL.

My UCL research asks who is able to benefit from a flexible, low-carbon energy system, on what terms, and how developments in AI might come to affect this.

Outside work I build and run various personal projects, which are more or less related to my research interests.

If you have an idea that sits across any of these, or an opportunity to exhibit, write or speak, please get in touch.
