MostMaker

your taste→somewhere to go in London

MostMaker recommends London restaurants, live music and theatre based on what you like, rather than what is most popular. You can describe your taste or bring in your fraglets. It can also find options for a group, picking places that suit everyone reasonably well rather than one person a lot.

AI assistants can use it directly, so you can ask yours to find somewhere for Friday and it will check with MostMaker.
